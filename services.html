Renovation and carpentry

We did the demo, framing, tile, flooring, siding, and trim on our own Cape Cod house. That house is our portfolio.

We are not booking renovation jobs yet. Send an inquiry and we will reach out as soon as we open the schedule.

What we build

  • Shower tile

    Prep, waterproofing, and tile for showers and tub surrounds, with clean lines and tight grout work.

  • Bathroom remodels

    Full gut-and-rebuild or a focused refresh, from framing to finish.

  • Painting and trim

    Interior painting, baseboard, casing, and crown, done to a finish you will notice.

  • Built-ins

    Media walls, shelving, closet systems, and islands, built to fit your room and your wood.

  • Outdoor work

    Fencing, decks, sheds, siding, and walkways built for Cape weather.

  • Custom furniture

    One-off tables, benches, and commissions in the same hand-finished style as our refinished pieces.

How pricing works

Every job is quoted as a single price after we see the space. No hourly surprises.
